## Specs

- **File**: `assets/backgrounds/<name>.jpg`, name exactly as below.
- **Size**: 1920 × 1080 (16:9) or larger, JPG, about 300–500 KB.
- **Composition**: the open book covers the middle of the screen (about 70% wide, 80% tall). Put the interesting shapes at the **left and right edges and in the sky**.
- **Dark edges**: a vignette is added, so dark foreground silhouettes (like the Manila one) work best.
- **Preview**: in the journal, press **B** to show which file the current scene uses.

Painterly digital oil painting, like the Manila Bay reference: warm golden-hour or lamplight against deep blue-grey shadow, dark silhouetted foreground shapes, soft atmospheric haze, visible brush texture. Muted teal / amber / umber palette. 1890s Philippines. No text, no logos, no modern objects, no recognisable real faces (figures stay as silhouettes or turned away).
